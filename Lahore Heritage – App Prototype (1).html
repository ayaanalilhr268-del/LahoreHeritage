About Us

Lahore Heritage is an authentic Pakistani restaurant set in a traditional Catalan masía just outside Barcelona. It brings the flavours of Lahore (karahis, kebabs, barbecue, dals and lassi) to the countryside of Rubí.

More than a restaurant, it is also a community centre, hosting weddings, events and family celebrations.
